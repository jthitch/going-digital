Taking the photo is only half the story. Editing is where your images come together, your style develops, and your work starts to look professional.

The Going Digital editing courses are designed to help photographers understand why they edit, not just which buttons to press. Whether you're fixing exposure, refining colour, or creating a polished final image, these courses give you the skills to edit with confidence.

We offer dedicated courses in Adobe Lightroom, Adobe Photoshop, and Affinity Photo, so you can learn on the platform that suits you best.

Explore Courses

What You'll Learn

Our photo editing courses cover the full workflow, from import to export. You'll learn how to work efficiently, make strong creative decisions, and produce consistent results across your images.

Topics include:

  • Understanding light, exposure, and colour in editing
  • Building a clean, repeatable editing workflow
  • Correcting exposure, contrast, and white balance
  • Colour grading and tonal control
  • Retouching portraits and landscapes
  • Sharpening images without overdoing it
  • Preparing images for print, web, and social media
  • Editing with intention, not guesswork

You'll see real examples throughout the course, including before and after edits, so you can clearly see how each adjustment affects the final image.

Lightroom Course

Our Lightroom editing course focuses on speed, consistency, and control.

You'll learn how to:

  • Import and organise your photo library
  • Use global and local adjustments effectively
  • Master colour, tone curves, and profiles
  • Create and use presets without damaging your images
  • Edit entire shoots efficiently

Ideal for photographers who want clean, natural edits and a fast workflow.

Photoshop Course

The Photoshop editing course goes deeper into precision and creative control.

You'll learn how to:

  • Work with layers and masks
  • Retouch portraits naturally
  • Remove distractions and refine details
  • Combine images and create composites
  • Use Photoshop alongside Lightroom

Perfect for photographers who want full control over every detail of their images.

Affinity Photo Course

The Affinity Photo course offers a powerful, affordable alternative to Adobe software.

You'll learn how to:

  • Navigate the Affinity Photo interface
  • Use adjustment layers and masks
  • Retouch and enhance images professionally
  • Build a complete editing workflow without subscriptions

A great choice for photographers looking for professional results using Affinity tools.

Learn by Seeing the Difference

Editing is best understood visually. That's why this page includes a before and after image slider, letting you see exactly how raw files are transformed through the editing process.

No tricks. No heavy-handed effects. Just thoughtful, professional photo editing.

Who These Courses Are For

  • Beginner photographers learning to edit for the first time
  • Hobbyists who want better-looking images
  • Professionals refining their workflow and style
  • Anyone frustrated by inconsistent edits

No matter your level, the focus is on practical skills you can use straight away.
