# Create a workshop

A **workshop** is a bookable instance of a course at a venue on a date (or open-dated / by arrangement).

## Who can do this

- **Super users** and **franchisees** (usually limited to their region’s venues/courses).

## What “done” looks like

1. Workshop is **Active** and linked to an **Active** course and venue.
2. Date (or open-dated) and **price** are set.
3. Tutor assigned when required.
4. It appears on the public course page and can be booked.
5. Optional: visible on the **[Workshop calendar]({{ urls.workshop_calendar }})**.

## Prerequisites

1. An approved, **Active** [venue]({{ urls.guide_create_venue }}).
2. An **Active** [course]({{ urls.guide_create_course }}).
3. A [tutor]({{ urls.tutor_changelist }}) record if you assign tutors.

## Steps

### 1. Open Workshops

Go to **[Workshops]({{ urls.workshop_changelist }})**, or use the **[Workshop calendar]({{ urls.workshop_calendar }})** for a date overview.

### 2. Add a workshop

Click **[Add workshop]({{ urls.workshop_add }})**.

Set:

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Course** | The product students book. |
| **Venue** | Where it runs (must be active/approved for public listing). |
| **Date** | Start date/time, or use **open dated** for date-by-arrangement / one-to-one. |
| **Price** | Student price in GBP. |
| **Tutor** | Who delivers the session. |
| **Active** | Off = hidden from booking. |
| **Max places** / enrolment | Capacity and whether booking is open. |
| **Strapline (Not displayed)** | Optional internal note — not shown to students. |
| **Byline** | Optional marketing text on the course listing and in emails. |

### 3. Documents & extras (optional)

- Workshop or venue joining documents may attach to confirmation emails when configured.
- Gallery images can be linked where the form allows.

### 4. Save

Save the workshop. Use **Duplicate workshop** on an existing workshop when you need another date with the same setup (then change the date).

### 5. Check the public site

1. Open the course on **[Photography courses]({{ urls.public_courses }})**.
2. Confirm the venue/date/price appear and **Book** works.
3. Check the venue page for “Upcoming courses” if the venue is public.
4. Location hubs under **[Courses by location]({{ urls.public_locations }})** update from bookable workshops.

## Tips

- Prefer the **calendar** view to spot clashes and empty months.
- Open-dated workshops are ideal for flexible one-to-one; labelled “Date by arrangement” on the site.
- Confirmation emails can also notify tutors, franchisees, and super users when that is enabled for the live site.

## Common mistakes

- Workshop on an **inactive** or **unapproved** venue — may not show publicly.
- Course inactive — workshop won’t book properly on the public site.
- Wrong region venue for a franchisee — may be blocked or invisible to that user.
- Zero/missing price — checkout will fail or look wrong.
