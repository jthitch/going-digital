# Workshop feedback

After a fixed-date workshop, students can receive a **day-after follow-up email** with a 1–5 star rating. Ratings and optional comments appear under **Workshop feedback** in admin.

## Who can do this

- **Everyone with admin access** — can **view** feedback for workshops they are allowed to see.
- **Franchisees** — see feedback for workshops in their area.
- **Super users** — see all feedback; only they can **delete** entries. Feedback is not edited in admin (students submit it).

Email wording for the follow-up is configured by **super users** under Website → Workshop follow-up email settings.

## What “done” looks like

1. Confirmed, paid students on a fixed-date workshop get a follow-up email the day after the workshop ends.
2. They click a star rating in the email.
3. **5 stars** — they are sent to leave a Google review.
4. **1–4 stars** — they can optionally leave written feedback on the site.
5. You can read ratings and comments in **[Workshop feedback]({{ urls.workshop_feedback_changelist }})**.

You do **not** create feedback yourself in admin — it appears when students use the email links.

## How follow-up emails are sent

The website sends these emails **automatically** once a day for workshops that **ended the day before**.

Emails only go to:

- bookings that are **confirmed** and **paid**,
- workshops with a **fixed date** (not open-dated / by arrangement),
- students who have an email address on the booking.

Each booking is only emailed once. If follow-up emails seem missing, contact a super user or site support to check that the daily email process is running, and that the booking meets the conditions above.

## Steps — review feedback in admin

### 1. Open Workshop feedback

Go to **Bookings → [Workshop feedback]({{ urls.workshop_feedback_changelist }})**.

### 2. Find ratings

- Filter by **rating** or **rated at** date.
- Search by student name, email, booking reference, or comment text.
- Open a row to see the full comment, booking, and workshop (all read-only).

### 3. Act on low scores

Use comments from 1–4 star ratings to follow up with the student or tutor outside this screen. There is no “reply” button in admin.

## What students experience

1. Email arrives with star links (1–5).
2. Clicking a star records the rating against their booking.
3. Five-star path → Google review page.
4. Lower ratings → optional comment form, then a thank-you page.

## Common mistakes

- **Looking for feedback the day of the workshop** — emails go out the **day after** the workshop end date.
- **Open-dated workshops** — no automatic follow-up email.
- **Trying to add or edit feedback in admin** — not supported; entries come from the student links only.
- **Assuming every booking will have feedback** — students may ignore the email or only click stars without a comment.
- **Franchisee missing a row** — you only see workshops in your region; ask a superuser for a full view.
